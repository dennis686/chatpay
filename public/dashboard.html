How you get paid

  1. The Chatpay team reviews your work.
  2. The amount appears in the table above as Pending.
  3. Chatpay sends it in Zambian kwacha to the Airtel Money or MTN MoMo number shown above.
  4. The status changes to Paid. If the money has not arrived, contact support with the date and amount.

To change your payout number, contact support. We will confirm it is really you before changing it.

Stay safe

  • A real employer never asks you to pay a fee to get a job or training.
  • Never share your mobile money PIN or the one-time code sent to your phone.
  • Get the pay, hours and employer name in writing before you start.
  • Start with one small paid task. Stop if the pay is delayed or the terms change.
